## LinkedIn

A private nursing home room in South Dakota runs $80,000 to $100,000 a year. Assisted living isn't much lighter, at $50,000 to $65,000. And Medicare won't cover any of it beyond short-term rehab after a hospital stay.

For farm families, that gap can force a hard choice: sell land or equipment to pay for care, or plan years ahead so it never comes to that. The right mix of long-term care insurance, VA Aid and Attendance benefits, and self-funded reserves depends on age, health, military service, and how much net worth is tied up in the operation.

Julie Underwood, CPA, and Brandi Altstaetter build this planning into the succession conversations they're already having with farm and professional service clients in Brookings. Waiting until your 80s to figure this out means most of your options are already gone.
