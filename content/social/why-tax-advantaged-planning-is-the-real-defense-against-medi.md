## LinkedIn

A knee replacement. A cancer diagnosis. A kid's emergency surgery. Nobody plans for these, but a savings account alone rarely covers them. Here's the gap: the IRS lets you deduct medical expenses over 7.5% of your AGI, but only if you itemize, and only if the timing works. Spread elective procedures across two years and you might clear that threshold in neither one. Bunch them into one year and you could unlock a real deduction.

For farm families on high-deductible health plans, an HSA adds a second layer: pre-tax contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals, up to $8,300 for family coverage in 2024. A bad crop year even changes how that 7.5% threshold behaves.

This is a conversation worth having in October, not April. By tax time, the timing decision is already gone.
