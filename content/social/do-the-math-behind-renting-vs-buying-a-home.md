## LinkedIn

A $60,000 down payment on a $300,000 Brookings home stops earning whatever it was earning before, a brokerage account, a farm operating line paydown, a business investment. That's opportunity cost, and most homeowners never put it on paper.

We walk clients through a five-year comparison: rent versus own, every line item included, from maintenance ($3,000-$6,000 a year) to the 8-10% transaction cost of buying and selling. Equity after five years often lands around $28,000-$35,000. Solid, but only if you can access it or you're staying put long enough to recover those costs.

For service business owners and agribusiness operators with stable cash flow, this framework matters as much as any equipment lease-or-buy decision. Run the real numbers before you sign anything.
