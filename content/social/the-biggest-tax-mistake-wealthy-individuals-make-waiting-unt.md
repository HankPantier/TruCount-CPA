## LinkedIn

By the first week of December, most of the year's income has already happened. That's the problem we see with high-net-worth clients and ag operators every year: the moves that actually change a tax bill, income timing, entity elections, funding a charitable vehicle, need months of lead time, not a folder of statements pulled together after Thanksgiving.

We've seen agribusiness operators save five figures a year in self-employment tax through an S corp election, but only because the change was in place before most of the income arrived. Wait until fall, and the year is already baked.

Farmers also leave real money on the table: the R&D credit for new seed varieties or irrigation techniques, and the excess fertility deduction on farmland purchases. Both require documentation as the year unfolds, not reconstruction the following April.

If your tax planning only happens in December, read this first: https://trucountcpa.com/resources/the-biggest-tax-mistake-wealthy-individuals-make-waiting-unt
