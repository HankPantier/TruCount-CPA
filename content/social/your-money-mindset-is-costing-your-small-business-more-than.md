## LinkedIn

A Brookings County farmer knew his equipment costs, grain prices, and input spend off the top of his head. He still avoided opening his year-end financials until his tax appointment forced it.

That's not a bookkeeping gap. It's a money mindset problem, and it costs more in missed deductions and underpriced work than most owners realize. We've seen it play out the same way in a multi-entity farm operation moving cash between three LLCs without tracking transfers, until intercompany accounting made the picture visible instead of vague.

Clean books help. But the habit of looking at them monthly, pricing off documented margins, and treating tax planning as routine rather than reactive is what actually changes cash flow outcomes.
