## LinkedIn

A $50,000 role that turns over can cost $15,000 to $20,000 in lost productivity before a replacement is fully trained. That number changes how you should weigh a $3,000 raise against a resignation letter. Wage growth that once forced 15% jumps to fill open roles is settling into steadier increases, and that shift belongs on the financial planning table, not just the HR one. Julie Underwood, CPA, walks clients through this exact math: tracking labor cost as a percentage of revenue, forecasting seasonal ag hiring before planting or harvest, and comparing retention costs against turnover costs instead of reacting hire by hire.
