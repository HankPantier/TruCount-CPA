## LinkedIn

Ten personal-use days can be the line between a rental loss you can deduct and one you can't. Rent your Lake Poinsett or Black Hills cabin more than 14 days a year, and the IRS math on personal-use versus rental days decides whether that property shelters income or just breaks even.

Cross into 'dwelling used as a home' territory (more than 14 personal days, or over 10% of rental days) and your deductions cap out at rental income. No loss allowed. Drop under that threshold and you're on Schedule E, with depreciation and potentially a $25,000 loss allowance against other income if your AGI is under $150,000.
