## LinkedIn

A ten-year-old combine bought for $420,000 isn't worth $420,000 today, but plenty of farm policies still insure it that way. Meanwhile, the six weeks of lost income if the one person running the planter gets hurt often has no coverage at all. Professional service firms have the mirror problem: a $1 million general liability policy sized to match a competitor's, while the partner who brings in 40 to 60 percent of billable revenue has no key person coverage behind them. Insurance decisions should follow your net worth and cash flow, not a renewal packet you skim once a year. Our new post walks through a four-part exposure check for property, liability, income replacement, and succession risk, plus how deductibility changes the real cost of each policy.
