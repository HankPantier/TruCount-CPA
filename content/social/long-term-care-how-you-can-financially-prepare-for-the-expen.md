## LinkedIn

A private room in a South Dakota nursing home runs $8,000 to $10,000 a month. Multiply that by two years and you're looking at a six-figure expense Medicare won't cover beyond a short post-hospital stay.

For farmers and agribusiness families, that number carries extra weight, since land and equipment make up most of the balance sheet and a care event can force a badly timed sale. Long-term care insurance is priced on age and health, so a 55-year-old in good health locks in a much lower premium than someone waiting until a diagnosis is already on file. Add the VA Aid and Attendance benefit for eligible veterans and surviving spouses, plus dedicated savings, and you've spread the risk across three sources instead of one.

Julie Underwood, CPA, and the TruCount CPA PC team walk clients through exactly this kind of planning alongside farm succession and retirement numbers, before it turns into a crisis.
