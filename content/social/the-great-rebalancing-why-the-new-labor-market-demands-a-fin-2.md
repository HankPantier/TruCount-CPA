## LinkedIn

A shop that raised its starting wage from $16 to $19 in 2022 to keep a crew together isn't seeing that number drop now that applications are back up. That's the real story of the labor market right now: wage growth is cooling, but the wages you raised during the panic years are permanent, and benefits and payroll taxes scale right along with them.

For farmers, agribusiness operators, and professional service firms, labor is usually the biggest controllable expense on the income statement, and most owners still manage it by feel instead of a monthly number. Replacing a skilled ag mechanic or an experienced bookkeeper commonly costs 20 to 30 percent of that role's annual pay once you count the ramp-up time, which makes a retention bonus look cheap by comparison.

Our advisory services build payroll and revenue analysis into your monthly numbers as standard, so you see a labor cost drift in June instead of March.
