## LinkedIn

Julie Underwood, CPA gets asked this every January: does my dog guarding the office under my desk count as a deduction? Almost always, no.

But the IRS does allow real deductions in specific cases: service dogs under Publication 502 (if you itemize and clear the 7.5% AGI threshold on medical expenses), guard dogs kept primarily at a business location under Publication 535, and working farm dogs used for herding or livestock protection.

The deduction usually gets denied for one reason: the dog lives a double life, guarding the shop by day and sleeping on the couch every night. If you can't separate personal use from business use, it doesn't hold up.

For Brookings and Kingsbury County farmers and business owners keeping working or guard dogs on-site, this is worth getting right before you file, not after.
