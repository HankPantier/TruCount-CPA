## LinkedIn

A client called our Brookings office asking if her Border Collie could be a write-off since the dog logs more hours on the farm than she does. The honest answer: usually no, unless the dog is doing real, documented work for a for-profit operation.

A livestock guardian dog protecting 60 head of cattle can qualify as a business expense on Schedule F. A guard dog watching a shop or ag supply yard works the same way. But a service dog is a different category entirely, deducted as a medical expense under IRS Publication 502, only above the 7.5% AGI floor. Mixed-use dogs need a documented split between personal and business time, the same way you'd track a truck used for both hauling grain and running errands.

Julie Underwood, CPA, walks multi-entity farm clients through exactly this kind of allocation. If you're weighing whether a working dog is worth the recordkeeping, read the full breakdown: https://trucountcpa.com/resources/unless-its-working-for-you-your-dog-isnt-a-tax-deduction-2
