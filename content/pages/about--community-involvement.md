## Why community roots matter to a Brookings CPA firm

Being a CPA firm in Brookings, SD means more than having a local mailing address. Kristy and Brandi have watched clients' operations grow, weather commodity price swings, and pass businesses down to the next generation. That kind of perspective doesn't come from a database. It comes from sitting across the table at the county fair or running into a client's daughter at a Chamber mixer.

TruCount CPA PC works with farmers and agribusiness operators across Brookings, Kingsbury, and Moody counties, alongside service-based business owners who show up to the same community events we do. When we talk through excess fertility deductions or R&D credits for a farm operation, we're not reciting tax code from a manual, we're applying it to soil types and equipment purchases we've already discussed over coffee. Local roots mean understanding the rhythm of planting season, calving season, and year-end deadlines, because we live on the same calendar our clients do.

## Supporting the people and events that shape our region

Sponsorship checks are easy to write. Showing up is harder, and it's what we'd rather be known for. Our team has sponsored the Brookings Figure Skating Club, supported Ag Women's Day programming for women in agriculture, and shown up at Brookings Area Chamber of Commerce events throughout the year. Not because it looks good on a website, but because these are the events our clients' families attend too.

- Brookings Figure Skating Club sponsorship, supporting local youth athletics
- Ag Women's Day, backing programming that builds financial confidence for women in agriculture
- Brookings Area Chamber of Commerce membership and event participation, staying connected to local business
- Everyday involvement in Brookings, Kingsbury, and Moody county activities where our clients live and work

## Recognized by the community we're proud to call home

Recognition from your own community carries weight. Those two recognitions sit alongside our standing with the AICPA and the South Dakota Society of CPAs, the professional bodies that hold Julie Underwood, CPA, and the rest of our team to a standard beyond ordinary bookkeeping.

## A team that's invested in your success, not just your spreadsheets

Kristy and Brandi have each spent 13 years with TruCount CPA PC, part of 51 years of combined accounting experience across the team. That kind of tenure means they've seen clients through drought years, expansion years, ownership transitions, and everything in between. It also means they know your operation well enough to catch a problem before it becomes one.

Brandi works with clients in Logan and Shelby Counties, Ohio, while the rest of the team is based in Brookings, serving South Dakota and Western Minnesota. Distance doesn't change how the firm operates. Every client gets a secure online platform for real-time collaboration, bundled services for one predictable monthly fee, and the kind of personalized, year-round tax advice that comes from a team that treats your numbers like their own family's.
