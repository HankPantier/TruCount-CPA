If your personal return is really a snapshot of your farm, your LLC, or your consulting business, a once-a-year drop-off with a seasonal preparer was never going to cut it. National chains and pop-up tax offices close their doors in April and reopen in January, leaving you to guess at your own numbers the other nine months. TruCount CPA PC works differently. Julie Underwood, CPA, and the team have spent 13 years in Brookings building relationships with farmers, agribusiness operators, and service business owners whose personal taxes are tied directly to how their operations perform. We know your Schedule F before you hand it over, because we've likely been reconciling your books all year. That familiarity is what catches deductions, flags problems early, and keeps April from ever feeling like a surprise.

## Built for farm families and business owners with complex returns

A personal return for someone running a grain operation, a service business, or a multi-entity farm structure looks nothing like a standard 1040. There's farm income and expense reporting, self-employment tax calculations, K-1s from partnerships, and depreciation schedules that all have to line up with what's happening on the business side. Miss the connection between the two, and you either overpay or invite a notice.

This is where niche experience pays off. TruCount works with farmers and agribusiness operators across Brookings, Kingsbury, and Moody counties, plus clients in western Minnesota and Logan and Shelby counties in Ohio, and that concentration means we know where the money hides. We look for opportunities like the R&D credit for qualifying farm activities and the excess fertility deduction on purchased farmland, credits and deductions that general preparers routinely overlook because they don't see enough ag returns to recognize them.

## Year-round tax planning, not just filing

Filing a return is a snapshot of decisions already made. Real tax planning happens before the year closes, while there's still time to act on it.

### Quarterly check-ins
We review income and expenses each quarter so estimated tax payments reflect what's actually happening in your operation, not a guess from last April.

### Deduction strategy sessions
Equipment purchases, retirement contributions, and entity structure all affect your personal liability. We flag opportunities before December 31, not after.

### Mid-year projections
A mid-year look at farm income or self-employment earnings shows whether you're on track or need to adjust withholding and payments.

### Filing with no surprises
By the time your return is prepared, you already know roughly what it will show. That's the point.

This is the personalized, year-round advice that separates a full-service relationship from a transaction, and it's why clients stay with TruCount for years instead of shopping for a new preparer every January.
