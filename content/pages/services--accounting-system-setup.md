## Get an accounting system built around how your business runs

If your books live in three different spreadsheets, or your QuickBooks file was set up by someone who left the company two years ago, you already know the cost. Missed transactions. Numbers that don't match the bank. Hours spent hunting for information that should take seconds to find. TruCount CPA PC has been solving this for Brookings, SD area farms, agribusinesses, and service companies since 2009. We don't hand you a generic template and wish you luck. We build a chart of accounts, software setup, and reporting structure around your actual operation, whether that's a grain and livestock farm in Kingsbury County or a professional services firm managing multiple client contracts. The goal is simple: a system you can trust the moment you open it.

## What our accounting system setup includes

A good setup isn't just picking software off a shelf. It's making sure every piece talks to the others, so your numbers are accurate the first time and every time after.

- Software selection matched to your business size, industry, and how you actually operate day to day
- Chart of accounts customization built around your revenue streams, cost centers, and entity structure
- Bank feed and payroll integration so transactions flow in automatically instead of getting keyed by hand
- Setup on TruCount's secure online platform, giving you real-time access and letting our team collaborate with you without waiting on emailed spreadsheets
- Reporting structure designed to answer the questions you actually ask, not generic templates nobody reads

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: farm-fields-accounting-records.jpg | alt: "Farmer reviewing financial records with crop fields visible outside" | query: "farmer reviewing paperwork office" -->
## Built for farm and agribusiness accounting realities

Farm accounting doesn't fit neatly into off-the-shelf software defaults. You need enterprise-level tracking that separates crop income from livestock income, equipment depreciation schedules that hold up at tax time, and cash flow reporting that accounts for the fact that most of your revenue lands in a six-week window each fall. Generic bookkeeping setups miss all of it. TruCount's ag clients across Brookings, Kingsbury, and Moody counties get a system built to track these realities from day one, along with year-round guidance on opportunities like the R&D credit for qualifying farm activities and the excess fertility deduction, both of which require the underlying accounting to be structured correctly to claim. If your current setup can't answer "which enterprise made money this year" in under a minute, it's not built for your operation.

## A system that fits service-based businesses too

Agriculture isn't the only client we build for. Professional services firms come to us with a different set of tangles: job costing that doesn't tie back to actual profitability, recurring billing that's tracked in someone's memory instead of the system, or multiple entities whose numbers need to stay separate but visible in one place. We set up your chart of accounts to track profitability by project, client, or service line, connect recurring billing so invoices go out on schedule, and structure multi-entity reporting so ownership sees the full picture without wading through five different logins. Whether you run a single-location practice or a small group of related companies, the system should make decisions easier, not harder.
