## Farm and agribusiness tax planning resources

Farmers and agribusiness operators face tax rules that never show up in a general small-business guide. These resources cover the credits and structures we see save real money for row-crop, livestock, and ag-service operations across South Dakota.

### R&D credit for farmers
photo: rd-credit-farm-fields.jpg
query: farmer walking crop field research

Plenty of farm operations qualify for the federal R&D credit through seed trials, irrigation testing, or new feed formulations, and never claim it. This guide walks through what counts as qualified research activity on a working farm.
[Read more](/industries/agriculture)

### Excess fertility deduction
photo: soil-testing-farm-field.jpg
query: soil testing farm field agriculture

If you bought land with above-average soil fertility already built in, part of that purchase price may be deductible as excess fertility rather than depreciated over decades. We explain how the deduction works and what documentation the IRS expects.
[Read more](/industries/agriculture)

### Multi-entity farm and service company structuring
photo: farm-business-paperwork-desk.jpg
query: farm business documents desk

Running the row-crop operation, the trucking arm, and the equipment LLC as separate entities can cut your tax bill and protect assets, but only when they're structured correctly from the start.
[Read more](/industries/agriculture/multi-entity-farm-tax-strategy)

### Multi-generational farm transition planning
photo: farm-family-generations-field.jpg
query: farm family generations outdoors

Passing the operation to the next generation without a tax bill that outlives the farm takes years of planning, not a single meeting the year someone decides to retire.
[Read more](/industries/agriculture/farm-succession-planning)

## Bookkeeping and payroll guides for service businesses

Service-based businesses, from law offices to engineering firms to salons, run into the same headaches: messy expense categorization, missed payroll tax deadlines, and accounting software that doesn't match how the business actually operates. These guides address the questions we get asked most, and tie into the day-to-day bookkeeping and payroll work handled for clients under one fixed monthly fee.

- Setting up expense categories that make tax time faster, not slower
- Payroll tax deposit deadlines and the penalty triggers small employers miss
- Choosing between cash and accrual accounting as a service business grows
- Picking accounting software that fits the business instead of forcing the owner to adapt to it

See how [bookkeeping](/services/bookkeeping), [payroll](/services/payroll), and [accounting system setup](/services/accounting-system-setup) work together under a single bundled fee.

## Business entity and retirement planning insights

Choosing an entity structure or a retirement plan isn't something an owner should have to guess at, or pay a lawyer by the hour to explain twice. These resources break down the decisions that come up most for growing farms and service businesses in Brookings and across South Dakota.

Our entity type analysis resources cover the real trade-offs between an LLC, S-corp, and C-corp for a farm operation or a service business, including the state and federal filings that follow whichever structure is chosen. On the retirement side, the guides look at SEP IRAs, SIMPLE IRAs, and 401(k) plans sized for a five-person shop or a family farm crew, not a Fortune 500 company, so an owner can offer a real benefit without overpaying an advisor by the hour.

Read more on [entity type analysis](/services/entity-type-analysis) and [retirement plan analysis](/services/retirement-plan-analysis).
