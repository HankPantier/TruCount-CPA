## A Brookings firm built around your peace of mind

TruCount CPA PC opened its doors in Brookings, South Dakota in 2009 with a straightforward goal: give farmers, agribusiness operators, and service-based business owners a clear, current picture of their numbers so surprises stop happening. Founder Julie Underwood, CPA, built the practice around a simple belief that business owners shouldn't have to guess where they stand financially. That belief still shapes how the firm works today.

Clients across Brookings, Kingsbury, and Moody counties come to TruCount for one reason: they want to spend less time buried in spreadsheets and more time running their operation. A dairy producer needs to know cash flow before planting season starts. A contractor needs payroll handled without a second thought. Both deserve numbers they can trust, delivered by people who know their name and their business.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: brookings-sd-office-history.jpg | alt: "Small CPA office team reviewing financial documents together at a desk" | query: "small accounting office team meeting" -->
## Our story from a solo practice to a trusted regional partner

Julie Underwood started TruCount CPA PC solo in 2009, doing the books, the taxes, and the client calls herself for the first four to five years. As the client list grew across South Dakota's ag country, so did the need for a full team, and the firm relocated to Brookings to be closer to the farmers and agribusiness operators it was built to serve.

That move set the stage for what TruCount is today: a team-based practice rather than a one-person shop, with the staying power to support clients through planting seasons, tax seasons, and everything between. TruCount now works with [agriculture businesses](/industries/agriculture) and service-based operations across Brookings, Kingsbury, and Moody counties, plus clients in Western Minnesota and Logan and Shelby Counties, Ohio. Sixteen years in, the firm has never stopped being local first.

## What makes TruCount different

Most accounting firms bill by the hour and hope you don't notice the invoice. TruCount works differently.

- Bundled services for one fixed monthly fee, so there's never a surprise bill for a phone call or a quick question
- A secure online platform where clients see their numbers and collaborate with the team in real time, not just at tax time
- Personalized, year-round tax and business advice instead of a single meeting each spring
- CFO-level insight into cash flow, entity structure, and planning decisions, delivered without the cost of hiring a full-time CFO

Clients who've worked with larger regional firms often notice the difference right away: someone at TruCount actually knows their operation, not just their file number.

## Our mission and where we're headed

TruCount CPA PC exists to give farmers, agribusiness operators, and service businesses peace of mind from knowing their numbers, so they can focus on operations, make confident decisions, and never get caught off guard by a number they should have seen coming.

The goal ahead is clear: to be the trusted financial partner for farmers, agribusiness operators, and service businesses across the Midwest, acting less like an outside vendor and more like an extension of each client's own team.
