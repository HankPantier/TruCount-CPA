# About TruCount CPA PC

## Identity
TruCount CPA PC was founded in 2009 in Brookings, South Dakota, initially run solo for four to five years before establishing its Brookings office. The firm is led by Julie Underwood, CPA, CEO, alongside a team including Brandi Altstaetter and Christy VanSickle — both with 13 years at the firm and part of a team with 51 years of collective accounting experience — plus staff accountants Cindy Halbersma, Lexy Spilde, and Amber Hanson.

Julie, Brandi, and Christy (referred to internally as Kristy) all grew up on farms, giving the team firsthand fluency in agricultural operations rather than secondhand textbook knowledge. The firm describes its culture as welcoming, supportive, knowledgeable, and family oriented, with staff deeply personally invested in clients' lives and families — often knowing them well enough that formal quarterly meetings become unnecessary.

Mission: To give farmers, agribusiness operators, and service businesses peace of mind from knowing their numbers so they can focus on operations, make confident decisions, and never be caught off guard.

Vision: To be the trusted financial partner for farmers, agribusiness operators, and service businesses across the Midwest, acting as an extension of their team.

The firm is affiliated with AICPA, the South Dakota Society of CPAs, and the Brookings Area Chamber of Commerce. Its office is located at 2301 Research Park Way, Suite #208, Brookings, SD 57006, serving Brookings, Kingsbury, and Moody counties in South Dakota, western Minnesota, and Logan and Shelby counties in Ohio.

## Positioning & Differentiation
TruCount's tagline is "Get out of the books and back to your business." Its positioning statement describes the firm as "full-service agriculture and professional services specialists who build deep relationships with their clients" — a blend of industry specialization and relationship-driven service.

Key differentiators include bundled services for a fixed monthly fee to avoid billing surprises, a secure online platform for real-time collaboration and data access, personalized year-round tax and business advice, and CFO-level business management insight at a fraction of the typical cost. Pricing typically follows three service package tiers, though the firm usually assesses client needs directly and provides a custom quote rather than presenting all three options upfront.

Clients most often find TruCount through referrals and online search. Their core need is consistent: wanting to know their numbers sooner rather than later so there are no surprises, and preferring full-service accounting relationships over one-off tax filing engagements.

Competitors in the Brookings market range from local full-service firms (Kinner & Company, Brian J. Hildebrant CPA, VRS CPA), a multi-location regional firm (ELO CPAs & Advisors), and national tax prep brands (H&R Block, Jackson Hewitt) to smaller local tax preparers (Hernandez Tax Service, Tax Consultants Inc.). TruCount differentiates through deep agricultural fluency, bundled monthly-fee packages, and a relationship-first approach rather than competing purely on service breadth or national brand recognition.

## Industries Served

### Farmers
TruCount helps farm operators cultivate a financially healthy enterprise with farm-fluent CPAs who understand ag-specific tax strategy. The core pain point: farmers can't find an accountant who understands agriculture or speaks their language. Notable tax opportunities the firm highlights for this niche include the R&D credit and the excess fertility deduction.

### Agribusiness Operators
TruCount provides full-service accounting and advisory for agribusiness operators managing complex multi-entity structures — such as combined farm and service-company setups. As with farmers, the primary pain point is difficulty finding an accountant who genuinely understands agriculture. The firm identifies opportunities in tax strategy content for multi-entity agribusiness structures and succession/exit planning for owners.

### Professional Services
TruCount takes the hassle out of complex accounting for professional service business owners who want a trusted financial partner, with a focus on sub-niches including chiropractors, architects, and consultants. The shared pain point across this niche: business owners are too busy running their practice to deal with bookkeeping, payroll, and tax, so accounting falls behind or gets ignored. A key tax strategy emphasized across these sub-niches is bonus depreciation — for example, guiding a chiropractor through the tax treatment of a handicapped table purchase, with potential R&D credit applicability as well.
